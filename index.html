📖 "Us Ne Block Kyun Kiya?" - Complete Story

Part 1: Shuruat

Roz raat 2 bajay tak baat hoti thi. "Good night" ke baad bhi 1 ghanta aur. Wo kehta tha "Tum meri chai ho. Subha tumhare message ke baghair din shuru nahi hota". Main pagal thi us ke liye. University, ghar, dost - sab bhool gayi thi.

Part 2: Badlao

Phir achanak replies late ane lagay. "Busy hun" ka label lag gaya har baat pe. Online hota tha, lekin mera message seen karke chor deta. Ek din poocha "Kya baat hai?" Kehta "Kuch nahi, bas kaam zyada hai". Lekin status pe quotes: "Move on".

Part 3: Block

Ek subha utha to WhatsApp pe sirf ek tick. DP ghayab. Number save tha lekin naam nahi aa raha tha. Block. Bina waja, bina laraai, bina alvida. 2 saal ki mohabbat ek click me khatam. 3 din tak khana nahi khaya. Ammi poochti thi "Beti kya hua?" Main kehti "Sir dard hai".

Part 4: Sabaq

6 mahine baad pata chala us ki mangni ho gayi thi. Ghar walon ne kahi aur rishta kar diya tha. Wo darr gaya tha. Mujhe batane ki himmat nahi thi, is liye block kar ke bhaag gaya. Sabaq: Jo chhor ke jaye, us ke liye kabhi apna aap mat maro. Jo sach me pyaar karta hai, wo darr ke bhaagta nahi, larta hai. Tumhari value block button se zyada hai 💕

Kya tumhare saath bhi aisa hua? WhatsApp pe batao. Naam nahi poochun gi.

📖 "Chai Wale Baba Ki Adhoori Mohabbat"

Baba Ka Thela

University ke bahar 70 saal ka baba chai bechta hai. Roz 5 bajay us ka thela lagta hai. Lekin roz shaam 7 bajay wo ek extra cup chai banata hai. Doodh patti wali, adrak daal ke. Phir 10 minute wait karta hai. Koi nahi aata. Phir wo chai khud pee leta hai aur thela band.

40 Saal Purana Raaz

Ek din himmat karke poocha "Baba ye extra chai kiske liye?" Baba hans pada. Bola "Beti, 1985 me yahan ek larki parhti thi. Roz shaam ko meri chai peene aati thi. Kehti thi 'Baba aapki chai me mohabbat hai'. Hum dost ban gaye. Phir pyaar ho gaya".

Bicharna

"Us ke abu ko pata chal gaya. Zabardasti shaadi karwa di Lahore me. Jaate hue boli thi 'Baba intezaar karna, main laut ke aaoongi'. 40 saal ho gaye beti. Wo nahi lauti. Lekin main roz us ke liye chai banata hun. Kya pata kab aa jaye. Mohabbat umeed ka naam hai".

Us din samjha mohabbat kya hoti hai. 40 saal ka intezaar. Bina shikayat, bina umeed chhore. Hum 2 din me move on kar lete hain. Baba 40 saal se usi bench pe baitha hai.

📖 "Long Distance Ke 7 Rules" - Complete Guide

Rule 1: Trust = Oxygen

Wo Dubai me, main yahan. 3 saal ho gaye. Pehle saal roz laraai. "Kahan ho? Kis ke saath ho?" Phir samjha - agar trust nahi to rishta zeher hai. Ab hum ek dosre ka phone nahi check karte. Kyunki jo sach me tumhara hai, wo 7000 km door bhi tumhara rahega.

Rule 2: Video Call = Mulaqat

Text me emotions nahi aate. "K" ka matlab "Theek hun" bhi ho sakta hai aur "Gussa hun" bhi. Roz 20 min video call zaruri. Chehra dekhna zaruri hai. Aankh sab sach bol deti hai.

Rule 3: Future Ki Baat

"Shaadi kab karni hai?" ye sawal se mat bhago. Date fix karo. "2 saal baad" bol ke chhor do. Goal ke baghair long distance rishta marathon hai jiska finish line nahi.

Baaki 4 rules agle hafte... Tab tak ye 3 follow karo. Rishta bach jayega ❤️
